# The Architecture of Intent
## Facilitator Coaching Script (120 Minutes)

Framework: C.L.E.A.R.+
Method references: Peak-End Rule, Serial Position Effect, Start/End Rituals, Visible Progress Tracking, Highlight Sessions.

---

## Segment 1 | Opening Ritual and Baseline
TIME: 00:00-00:15 | 15 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Welcome to The Architecture of Intent. Quick check-in ritual: in one sentence, say your name, your goal for today, and one place AI has frustrated you. We are here to turn that frustration into a repeatable method.

DO (Facilitation Actions):
- Stand center and run low-friction check-in immediately.
- On whiteboard create three columns: Name, Goal, AI Friction.
- Capture entries in real time and keep pace high.
- At minute 12, read back the top patterns and where each will be solved.

ASK (Audience Prompts):
- What is your honest gut-feeling about LLM output quality right now?
- What is one result you could not trust?
- Which part of your workflow loses the most time to AI rework?

CAPTURE and ADAPT:
- Tag each friction point with a segment destination (Delegation Lab, Makeover, Iteration).
- Select two frequent pain points for later live case studies.

FACILITATION RITUAL / PRO-TIP:
- Serial Position + Strong Start Ritual: break silence in the first 90 seconds.
- Keep each share short to build momentum and safety.

---

## Segment 2 | Human Delegation Lab
TIME: 00:15-00:35 | 20 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Round one starts now: Architect gives one instruction only. Builder executes with no questions and no gestures from Architect. We run this on three everyday frames: Tree Climbing safety instruction, interpersonal conflict resolution message, and shared responsibility handoff. Then we reveal the assumption gap. Round two repeats with C.L.E.A.R.+.

DO (Facilitation Actions):
- Pair participants and assign roles.
- Enforce strict Round 1 rule: no gestures and no questions.
- Timer split: 7 minutes Round 1, 8 minutes Round 2, 5 minutes reveal.
- Rotate all pairs through Tree Climbing, interpersonal conflict, and shared responsibility frames.
- After debrief, visibly tick Segment 2 complete on the board.

ASK (Audience Prompts):
- Architects: what did you assume was obvious?
- Builders: what did you have to invent?
- Group: where did quality fail first, scope, format, or audience fit?
- Which frame broke first and why: Tree Climbing, interpersonal conflict, or shared responsibility handoff?

CAPTURE and ADAPT:
- Capture one failure snapshot per framing example and label each by failure type.
- Reuse Tree Climbing in Segment 5 and shared responsibility in Segment 6.

FACILITATION RITUAL / PRO-TIP:
- Visible Progress Tracking Ritual: mark completion publicly to create momentum.

---

## Segment 3 | Assumption Gap and C.L.E.A.R.+ Engine
TIME: 00:35-00:50 | 15 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Now we deconstruct the gap. Weak outputs usually come from weak instructions. We tighten context, limits, output shape, audience, role, and verification until the result becomes trustworthy. We compare Tree Climbing, interpersonal conflict, and shared responsibility to show how meaning shifts when role and audience change.

DO (Facilitation Actions):
- Use one participant example from opening board.
- Rebuild it field by field through C, L, E, A, R, and plus.
- Rebuild one Tree Climbing, one conflict-resolution, and one shared-responsibility prompt.
- Keep movement active and point physically to each field as you add it.

ASK (Audience Prompts):
- Which field changed usefulness most?
- Which field do you skip under time pressure?
- What exclusion should be mandatory in your domain?
- Which framing example made hidden assumptions easiest to detect?

CAPTURE and ADAPT:
- Convert one participant prompt and one shared-responsibility prompt into reusable scaffolds.
- Save Tree Climbing scaffold as the first task in Segment 5.

FACILITATION RITUAL / PRO-TIP:
- Serial Position reinforcement: make this midpoint memorable with one dramatic before/after delta.

---

## Segment 4 | Break and Informal Table Chat
TIME: 00:50-01:00 | 10 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Break now. Step away from screens, grab coffee, and reset your brain. At your table discuss one insight and one unresolved challenge. We restart in exactly ten minutes.

DO (Facilitation Actions):
- Start visible 10-minute timer.
- Walk room to prevent laptop creep.
- At 00:59, give one-minute warning and request one spokesperson per table.

ASK (Audience Prompts):
- On return: what challenge should we tackle first next?

CAPTURE and ADAPT:
- Capture one challenge per table.
- Assign each challenge to a triad before Segment 5 begins.

FACILITATION RITUAL / PRO-TIP:
- Recovery Ritual: protect the break as non-negotiable for second-half energy.

---

## Segment 5 | Prompt Makeover Studio
TIME: 01:00-01:25 | 25 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Prompt Makeover Studio starts with your real problems. No generic templates. Triads rotate roles every eight minutes and produce one prompt that can survive public critique.

DO (Facilitation Actions):
- Assign triads to participant-owned cases.
- Rotate roles at minute 8 and 16.
- Start critique at minute 22.
- After critique, visibly tick Segment 4 complete on the board.

ASK (Audience Prompts):
- Which constraint removed the most fluff?
- Where is the output still vague?
- What makes this prompt shippable today?

CAPTURE and ADAPT:
- Replace all default examples with participant cases.
- Harvest one strong and one weak version for Segment 6 live debug.

FACILITATION RITUAL / PRO-TIP:
- Visible Progress + Dynamic Harvest Ritual: publicly mark progress and reinforce participant ownership.

---

## Segment 6 | Live Iteration Crucible
TIME: 01:25-01:50 | 25 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Live Iteration Crucible now. One variable per turn. Diagnose the failure, isolate one change, then constrain with sharper exclusions and pass-fail checks.

DO (Facilitation Actions):
- 10 minutes solo build, 10 minutes peer audit, 5 minutes final patch.
- Use one harvested weak-vs-strong pair from Segment 5 for live debugging.
- Tick Segment 5 complete on board at minute 49.

ASK (Audience Prompts):
- What failed and why?
- What assumption still leaks?
- Which rejection test catches this failure next time?

CAPTURE and ADAPT:
- Run two live iterations on participant case pair.
- Require evidence from revision logs before accepting final patch.

FACILITATION RITUAL / PRO-TIP:
- Visible Progress Tracking Ritual: public completion mark before final close.

---

## Segment 7 | Highlight Session and Commitments
TIME: 01:50-02:00 | 10 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Final Highlight Session. Round-robin: each person shares one big win and one personal commitment. Tell us your before-and-after prompt transformation in one sentence.

DO (Facilitation Actions):
- 4-minute commitment writing.
- 4-minute round-robin highlights.
- 2-minute close with progress summary from board.
- End exactly on time.

ASK (Audience Prompts):
- What is your one big win?
- What changed from your before prompt to your after prompt?
- What commitment will you execute this week and who will hold you accountable?

CAPTURE and ADAPT:
- Map each commitment to one opening friction point.
- Summarize group progress and remaining challenges.

FACILITATION RITUAL / PRO-TIP:
- Peak-End Rule + Highlight Session Ritual: make the ending emotionally vivid and action-oriented for retention.

Close line:
Reliable AI outcomes are not luck. They come from intentional instructions, visible progress, and disciplined iteration.
