# The Architecture of Intent
## Interactive Facilitator Coaching Script (120 Minutes)

Audience: founders, students, operators, and teams building real AI-assisted workflows.
Framework: C.L.E.A.R.+

---

## Segment 1 | Opening and Contract
TIME: 00:00-00:08 | 8 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Welcome to The Architecture of Intent. You are in the right room if AI has ever given you polished nonsense. Today we fix that with a practical system you can use tomorrow.

DO (Facilitator Staging and Movement):
- Stand center stage and slow your pacing for the first minute.
- Establish psychological safety: mistakes are expected and useful.
- Write workshop contract on whiteboard: one-variable iteration, explicit exclusions, peer audit before final output.

ASK (Interactive Questions):
- Go around the room: state your name, your primary goal for today, and your current gut-feeling about AI.
- What is one output you regret trusting?

CAPTURE and ADAPT (Dynamic Example Extraction):
- Create two columns on flipchart: Participant Goals and Pain Points.
- Capture exact participant phrases, not summaries.
- Tag each pain point with M (Makeover) or I (Iteration) to reuse later.

FACILITATOR PRO-TIPS (Energy and Engagement):
- If energy is low, share your own AI miss first to model honesty.
- Keep intros to 20 seconds each and enforce time gently.

Transition bridge:
We now map the whole session so you can see where your challenge gets solved.

---

## Segment 2 | Agenda Activation
TIME: 00:08-00:15 | 7 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Here is the map. We front-load diagnosis, protect a mandatory rest break, and spend the biggest blocks doing live collaboration. This is not a lecture marathon; this is skill practice.

DO (Facilitator Staging and Movement):
- Walk left to right while pointing at each agenda block.
- Mark transition times visibly: 00:15, 00:50, 01:00, 01:25, 01:50.

ASK (Interactive Questions):
- What part of this agenda feels hardest for you right now?
- Who here has never run a peer prompt audit before?

CAPTURE and ADAPT (Dynamic Example Extraction):
- Note where most hands go (constraints, iteration, confidence).
- If iteration fear is highest, commit to extra live debug in Segment 6.
- If constraints fear is highest, preselect two opening pain points for Segment 5.

FACILITATOR PRO-TIPS (Energy and Engagement):
- Keep this section fast and hopeful.
- Park deep theory questions publicly and return in Segment 4.

Transition bridge:
Now we expose the assumption gap directly through a paired simulation.

---

## Segment 3 | Human Delegation Lab
TIME: 00:15-00:35 | 20 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Round one now: Architect gives instructions with no gestures and no clarifying questions from Builder. We want to expose the assumption gap, not hide it. Round two re-runs the same task with C.L.E.A.R.+ and we compare outcomes.

DO (Facilitator Staging and Movement):
- Form pairs and assign Architect and Builder roles.
- Enforce Round 1 rule strictly: no gestures, no hints, no questions.
- Pause after Round 1 reveal and have Builders read assumptions aloud.
- Start Round 2 with one clarifying question max.

ASK (Interactive Questions):
- Architects: what did you think was obvious that was not obvious?
- Builders: what did you have to guess?
- Group: where did quality drop first, scope or output shape?

CAPTURE and ADAPT (Dynamic Example Extraction):
- Capture three assumption-gap examples using participant wording.
- Ensure one example comes from a quiet table.
- Flag these examples for Segment 5 makeover critiques.

FACILITATOR PRO-TIPS (Energy and Engagement):
- Walk to the quietest table first.
- If participants start helping each other in Round 1, reset and restart timer.

Transition bridge:
Now we deconstruct why those misses happened and how to prevent them.

---

## Segment 4 | Assumption Gap and C.L.E.A.R.+ Engine
TIME: 00:35-00:50 | 15 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Weak output is usually not model stupidity; it is instruction ambiguity. C sets destination, L sets guardrails, E shapes deliverable, A tunes voice, R controls process, and plus verifies quality before acceptance.

DO (Facilitator Staging and Movement):
- Use one real participant prompt from opening board.
- Add one C.L.E.A.R.+ field at a time and read output deltas.
- Physically point to board columns when adding constraints and output shape.

ASK (Interactive Questions):
- Which field changed usefulness the most?
- Which field do you usually skip?
- What exclusion should be non-negotiable in your domain?

CAPTURE and ADAPT (Dynamic Example Extraction):
- Convert one M-tagged pain point into a full C.L.E.A.R.+ scaffold live.
- Save scaffold as baseline for Segment 5 triads.

FACILITATOR PRO-TIPS (Energy and Engagement):
- Show deltas quickly and ask the room to name the pattern.
- Resist over-teaching definitions; prioritize visible transformation.

Transition bridge:
Break now. Return ready to apply this on your own real cases.

---

## Segment 5 | Break and Informal Table Chat
TIME: 00:50-01:00 | 10 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Break time. Please step away from screens, grab coffee or water, and move your body. At your table, discuss one insight and one unresolved challenge. We restart sharply in ten minutes with your own real examples.

DO (Facilitator Staging and Movement):
- Start a visible 10-minute timer.
- Walk tables and ensure people disengage from laptops.
- At 00:59 announce one-minute warning and ask for table spokesperson.

ASK (Interactive Questions):
- On return cue: what is the one challenge your table wants solved first next?

CAPTURE and ADAPT (Dynamic Example Extraction):
- Capture one challenge per table at restart.
- Map each challenge to a triad in Segment 6.
- Prioritize unresolved opening pain points over prepared demos.

FACILITATOR PRO-TIPS (Energy and Engagement):
- Protect the break; do not run mini-consulting during pause.
- Save answers for Segment 6 so the room learns together.

Transition bridge:
Now we run makeover triads using your real challenges, not generic prompts.

---

## Segment 6 | Prompt Makeover Studio
TIME: 01:00-01:25 | 25 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Now we run Prompt Makeover Studio using your problems, not generic demos. Triads rotate through author, constraints auditor, and output auditor every eight minutes. Your goal is to produce one prompt that survives peer critique.

DO (Facilitator Staging and Movement):
- Assign triads and map each triad to one captured participant challenge.
- Run 8-minute timer and rotate roles at minute 8 and minute 16.
- At minute 22 run room-wide critique with one quiet-table case and one vocal-table case.

ASK (Interactive Questions):
- Which constraint most reduced fluff?
- Where is the output still underspecified?
- What makes this prompt execution-grade rather than request-grade?

CAPTURE and ADAPT (Dynamic Example Extraction):
- Replace default examples with the two strongest participant-owned cases.
- Extract reusable improvement patterns and write them on board for Segment 7.

FACILITATOR PRO-TIPS (Energy and Engagement):
- Alternate speaking turns across tables to avoid dominance.
- Call on quieter groups first to increase inclusion and confidence.

Transition bridge:
Now we pressure-test your prompts in multi-turn debugging.

---

## Segment 7 | Live Iteration Crucible
TIME: 01:25-01:50 | 25 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Live Iteration Crucible starts now. Keep your revision log visible. Each turn changes exactly one variable: diagnose the failure, isolate the change, then constrain with sharper exclusions and acceptance checks.

DO (Facilitator Staging and Movement):
- Run 10-minute solo build, 10-minute peer audit, 5-minute final patch.
- Move table to table and ask for evidence from revision logs, not opinions.
- At minute 40 run one whole-room debug on an unresolved participant case.

ASK (Interactive Questions):
- Builders: what failed and why?
- Auditors: what assumption still leaks?
- Both: what rejection test will catch this failure next time?

CAPTURE and ADAPT (Dynamic Example Extraction):
- Select one unresolved case from Segment 6.
- Demonstrate two live iterations with pass-fail checks.
- Ask room to vote on stronger patch and justify with evidence.

FACILITATOR PRO-TIPS (Energy and Engagement):
- If energy drops, run a 20-second stand-up checkpoint from each table.
- Keep countdown cues visible and strict.

Transition bridge:
Final sprint. Convert learning into a personal execution contract.

---

## Segment 8 | Synthesis and Commitment Contract
TIME: 01:50-02:00 | 10 min

SAY (Verbatim Script):
Final sprint. Write your personal commitment contract now: one real task you will refactor this week, two exclusions you will always include, and one pass-fail rule you will enforce before shipping output.

DO (Facilitator Staging and Movement):
- Start 4-minute writing timer.
- Run 3-minute pair share.
- Run 3-minute confidence re-poll and close exactly on time.
- Collect one commitment card per table and read two aloud.

ASK (Interactive Questions):
- What changed in your confidence from start to now?
- What is your non-negotiable exclusion from today forward?
- Who will review your first contract this week?

CAPTURE and ADAPT (Dynamic Example Extraction):
- Match each table commitment to one opening pain point.
- Close loop explicitly: this goal is now tied to this behavior change.

FACILITATOR PRO-TIPS (Energy and Engagement):
- End with precision, not length.
- Thank participants by naming behaviors you observed: clearer constraints, stronger audits, cleaner revisions.

Close line:
Reliable AI output is not luck. It is communication architecture plus disciplined iteration.
