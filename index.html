The Human Delegation Lab

Architect and Builder paired simulation with before/after structure comparison.

Round 1: Raw Request

  • No gestures.
  • No clarifying questions.
  • Surface assumptions in plain language.

Round 2: Structured Request

  • Rewrite with C.L.E.A.R.+ fields.
  • Allow one clarifying question.
  • Compare rework and confidence delta.

Live Iteration Crucible

Exercise D: Solo build, peer audit, and multi-turn debugging on real participant cases.

Diagnose-Isolate-Constrain Loop

  1. Diagnose: identify the failed C.L.E.A.R.+ field.
  2. Isolate: modify one variable only.
  3. Constrain: add explicit exclusions and acceptance checks.

Peer audit: "What assumption remains unconstrained and what check rejects it?"
