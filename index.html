Prompt Makeover Studio

Exercise C: Triad refactoring and live critique.

Before

  • No output structure
  • No explicit exclusions

After (C.L.E.A.R.+)

Limits: no paid ads, no legal claims, no fabricated data.
Expected: week-by-week action table, owners, and risk log.

Live Iteration Crucible

Exercise D: Solo build, peer audit, and multi-turn debugging.

Diagnose-Isolate-Constrain Loop

  1. Diagnose: identify the failed C.L.E.A.R.+ field.
  2. Isolate: modify one variable only.
  3. Constrain: add explicit exclusions and acceptance checks.

Peer audit: "What assumption remains unconstrained and what check rejects it?"

TIME 01:25-01:50 | SAY: Run multi-turn debugging with one-variable edits only. DO: require revision logs and enforce peer audit criteria before final run.
