C.L.E.A.R.+ Desk Reference

For shared meaning between humans and AI: make context, constraints, output, audience, process, and verification explicit before execution.

The Protocol

CContext and goal: situation, source material, objective, success condition.
LLimits and exclusions: boundaries, non-goals, risks, and forbidden moves.
EExpected output: format, sections, length, evidence, decision use.
AAudience: reader, expertise, tone, assumptions, accessibility.
RRole/Process: stance, expertise, steps, reasoning method.
+Iterate/Verify: failure signals, revision rule, pass-fail checks.

Steering Loop

  1. Diagnose: name the failed C.L.E.A.R.+ field.
  2. Isolate: change one variable only.
  3. Constrain: add exclusions and acceptance tests.

Peer Rubric

  • Context is specific.
  • Limits include explicit exclusions.
  • Expected output is structured.
  • Audience is named.
  • Role/process shapes the work.
  • Verification can reject a bad answer.

Zero-Trust Data Rules

  • Do not invent facts, citations, or numbers.
  • Do not paste confidential data into prompts.
  • Do not make claims without traceable evidence.
  • When evidence is missing, state the gap.
