C.L.E.A.R.+ Prompting Desk Card

Professional Prompting & Human Communication Workshop • 90-Minute Edition

C.L.E.A.R.+ Breakdown

  • C | Context & Goal: objective, business setting, completion definition.
  • L | Limits & Negative Constraints: boundaries, exclusions, non-negotiables.
  • E | Expected Output: required format, sections, length, fields.
  • A | Audience: who will consume output, domain literacy, tone target.
  • R | Role & Process: model persona and step-by-step method.
  • + | Iteration/Verification: test criteria and revision loop.

3-Step Iteration Loop

Diagnose
  • Identify failing field in C.L.E.A.R.+.
  • Pinpoint assumption causing drift.
Isolate
  • Change one variable only per retry.
  • Keep the rest of the prompt fixed.
Constrain
  • Add explicit do-not-do clauses.
  • Define pass/fail checks before rerun.

Negative Constraint Patterns

  • No fabricated data, references, citations, or legal claims.
  • No scope expansion beyond provided product phase.
  • No implementation details that require unstated dependencies.
  • No confidential or personal data in examples.
  • No markdown tables unless explicitly requested.

Pre-Flight Checklist

  • Goal measurable in one sentence.
  • Constraints include at least two exclusions.
  • Output format is concrete and reviewable.
  • Audience and reading level specified.
  • Verification rubric included.

Reusable Prompt Skeleton

Role: [who the model is and how it should think] Context & Goal: [problem + desired outcome] Limits: [rules + explicit exclusions] Expected Output: [format + sections + length] Audience: [target reader and tone] Verification: [checks, edge cases, confidence notes]

Facilitator Cue Card

Session Cadence
  • Every 10-15 minutes: ask "What assumption is still unconstrained?"
  • Before every rerun: document one change only.
  • At close: each participant commits one workplace application.
Timing Guardrail

  • 01:20 mark: start post-test and action planning.
