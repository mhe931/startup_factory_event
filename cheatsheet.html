Ritual Stack (AJ&Smart Inspired)

Start Ritual
  • Low-friction check-in in first 90 seconds.
  • Write names, goals, and AI friction points live.
Visible Progress
  • Tick completed blocks after Segments 2, 5, and 6.
Peak-End
  • Final round-robin: One Big Win and Commitment.

Dynamic Example Harvesting

  • Capture participant pain points in Segment 1.
  • Assign table challenges after break.
  • Replace generic demos with participant cases in Segments 5 and 6.

Multi-Angle Framing Examples

  • Tree Climbing: same goal, different audience and safety constraints.
  • Interpersonal Conflict: same issue, different tone and repair objective.
  • Shared Responsibility: same outcome, different ownership language.

Iteration Loop

  • Diagnose: Find failure field.
  • Isolate: Change one variable only.
  • Constrain: Add explicit exclusions and tests.

Facilitator Rules

Energy
  • Do not speak over small-group chatter.
  • Use visible timers for every block.
Clarity
  • One instruction at a time.
  • Require evidence from revision logs.
